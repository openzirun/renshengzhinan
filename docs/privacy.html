隐私政策 · Privacy Policy

适用范围：人生指南 iOS/iPadOS 应用及今日一读小组件。生效日期：2026-10-08。English version

1. 我们处理哪些数据

应用无需注册或登录。应用本身不向开发者服务器发送个人资料、阅读记录或设备标识，不包含广告、第三方分析或用户跟踪 SDK。我们不通过应用收集或出售个人数据。

2. 设备上的本地数据

应用使用设备上的本地存储保存语言选择、字号、收藏、已读记录、行动清单及完成状态。这些数据用于提供阅读功能，不上传到开发者服务器。主应用和小组件仅通过设备上的 App Group 共享语言设置。

你可在应用中取消收藏、删除行动及修改阅读设置。删除应用通常会移除其本地应用数据；iOS 的系统备份或恢复机制可能保留或恢复数据，具体取决于你的设备与 Apple 备份设置。本应用不提供自己的账号、服务器同步或云端数据库。

3. 网络连接与外部链接

正文随应用提供，可离线阅读。打开引用来源、项目网站或支持页面时，会通过系统浏览器访问第三方网站。第三方网站可能按其隐私政策处理 IP 地址、浏览器信息或 Cookie；这不属于应用内的跟踪功能。

4. App Store 付款与系统服务

应用下载与付款由 Apple App Store 处理。开发者不会通过本应用获取你的支付卡资料。Apple 可能按其政策向开发者提供商店统计或你选择共享的诊断信息。请参阅 Apple 隐私政策。

5. 主动联系支持

如果你通过 GitHub Issues 联系维护者，你提交的文字、截图和账号信息会由 GitHub 处理。公开 Issue 对所有人可见，请勿附上电话、密码、支付信息、医疗记录或其他敏感资料。我们仅使用你主动提供的信息处理支持请求，不用于广告。你可按 GitHub 的功能编辑或删除自己发布的内容。参阅 GitHub 隐私声明。

6. 本网站

这些文档由 GitHub Pages 托管，没有内嵌广告、分析脚本、表单或自设跟踪 Cookie。GitHub 可能为提供和保护服务处理访问日志，包括 IP 地址。

7. 政策变更与联系

应用的数据处理方式发生变化时，我们会更新本页及生效日期。隐私疑问请通过项目支持入口联系维护者。

Privacy Policy — English

Effective October 8, 2026. Applies to Better Life Guide for iOS/iPadOS and its daily reading widget.

App data

No account is required. The app does not transmit personal information, reading history or device identifiers to developer-operated servers. It has no advertising, third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not collect or sell personal data through the app.

Local storage

Language, reading size, bookmarks, read history, action items and completion status are stored locally to provide app features. Only the language setting is shared between the app and widget through an on-device App Group. You can remove bookmarks and action items or change settings in the app. Deleting the app generally removes its local app data; iOS backup and restore may retain or restore data according to your Apple and device settings. The app has no developer-operated account, cloud database or synchronization service.

External services and purchases

Reading content is bundled for offline use. Source and support links open third-party websites in the system browser; those websites may process IP addresses, browser information and cookies under their own policies. Apple handles App Store downloads and payments. The app does not receive payment card details. Apple may provide store statistics or diagnostics you choose to share, under Apple’s privacy policy.

Support and this website

Information you voluntarily submit to GitHub Issues is processed by GitHub and used by maintainers to address your request. Public issues are visible to everyone: do not post sensitive information. You can edit or delete your own posts using GitHub’s controls. This GitHub Pages website has no ads, analytics scripts, forms or custom tracking cookies; GitHub may process access logs, including IP addresses, to operate and protect the service. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy and its effective date if our data practices change. Contact maintainers through the project support page with privacy questions.
